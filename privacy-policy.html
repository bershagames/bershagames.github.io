Block Merge Rush Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 10, 2026 · Version 1.0.0

1. Local game data

Block Merge Rush stores progress, settings, scores, in-game currencies, cosmetics and related gameplay data locally on your device. BERSHA GAMES does not operate a game server that receives this local gameplay data in version 1.0.0.

2. Online accounts and cloud services

Google Play Games sign-in, cloud save and online leaderboards are not active in version 1.0.0.

3. Advertising and rewarded ads

Block Merge Rush includes Google Mobile Ads technology for optional rewarded advertisements. A player may choose to watch a rewarded ad to receive an in-game reward. The current game design grants 1 Nova Gem after a rewarded ad is successfully completed, subject to an in-game daily limit of 3 rewarded Gem ads.

Google's advertising services may process technical and advertising-related information such as device and app information, IP/network information, advertising identifiers when available and permitted, ad impressions and interactions, diagnostics, security and fraud-prevention signals. The exact data processed can depend on the device, region, consent choices and Google service configuration.

4. Consent and privacy choices

Where required, the app uses Google's consent flow before requesting ads. Advertising is requested only when the consent state allows ads to be requested. The app may use non-personalized-ad settings depending on consent and release configuration.

5. Real-money purchases

Version 1.0.0 does not process real-money purchases. Nova Gems earned through rewarded ads are in-game virtual currency and are not purchased with money in this version.

6. Sharing and third parties

BERSHA GAMES does not intentionally upload your locally stored game progress to its own server. Advertising-related data may be processed by Google as part of Google Mobile Ads. Google's handling of that information is governed by Google's applicable privacy and advertising policies.

7. Backup and sharing

If you choose to export a game backup, Android may open its standard file picker or share interface so you can select where to save or send the file. The selected destination is handled by Android and the app or service you choose.

8. Retention and deletion

Local game data remains on your device until you delete or reset it. You can remove local game data using the in-game privacy/data controls or Android app storage settings. See the Data Deletion page for details. Data processed by Google services is subject to Google's own retention and deletion practices.

9. Sensitive device permissions

Core gameplay does not require precise location, contacts, camera or microphone access. Advertising SDK components may use permissions or identifiers provided by Android and Google Play where permitted.

10. Changes

This policy will be updated when the app's services or data practices materially change.
