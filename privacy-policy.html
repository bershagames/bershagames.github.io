Block Merge Rush Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 10, 2026 · Version 1.0.0

1. Game progress and local data

Block Merge Rush stores game progress, settings, scores, in-game currencies, cosmetics and related gameplay data locally on your device. BERSHA GAMES does not operate a server that receives this local gameplay data in version 1.0.0.

2. Online services

Google Play Games sign-in, cloud save and online leaderboards are not active in version 1.0.0.

3. Advertising

Version 1.0.0 does not contain an active advertising SDK and does not display ads.

4. Purchases

Version 1.0.0 does not process real-money purchases. In-game currencies and cosmetic items available in this version are not purchased with real money.

5. Data sharing

BERSHA GAMES does not intentionally upload or share your locally stored Block Merge Rush gameplay data with third parties in version 1.0.0.

6. Backup and sharing

If you choose to export a game backup, Android may open its standard file picker or share interface so that you can choose where to save or send the file. Your choice of destination is handled by Android and the app or service you select.

7. Data retention and deletion

Local game data remains on your device until you delete or reset it. You can remove local game data using the in-game privacy/data controls or Android's app storage settings. See the Data Deletion page for details.

8. Children and sensitive information

Block Merge Rush does not require precise location, phone number, contacts, camera or microphone access for its core gameplay in version 1.0.0.

9. Changes to this policy

If a future version introduces online accounts, cloud services, advertising, real-money purchases or other services that process additional data, this Privacy Policy will be updated to reflect those changes.
