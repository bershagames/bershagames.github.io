Block Merge Rush Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 10, 2026 · Version 1.0.0

1. Local game data

Block Merge Rush stores progress, settings, scores, in-game currencies, cosmetics and related gameplay data locally on your device. BERSHA GAMES does not operate a game server that receives this local gameplay data in version 1.0.0.

2. Online accounts and cloud services

Google Play Games sign-in, cloud save and online leaderboards are not active in version 1.0.0.

3. Advertising and rewarded ads

Block Merge Rush includes Google Mobile Ads technology for optional rewarded advertisements. A player may choose to watch a rewarded ad to receive 1 Nova Gem after successful completion, subject to an in-game daily limit of 3 rewarded Gem ads.

Google advertising services may process device/app information, IP/network information, advertising identifiers when available and permitted, ad impressions and interactions, diagnostics, security and fraud-prevention signals. Exact processing depends on device, region, consent choices and Google service configuration.

4. Consent and age-appropriate ads

Where required, the app uses Google's consent flow before requesting ads. Rewarded ads are configured as non-personalized and restricted to age-appropriate content. The app's ad request configuration uses child-directed/under-age treatment and a General maximum ad content rating for its declared mixed 6+ audience.

5. Real-money purchases

Block Merge Rush offers optional Nova Gems packages as consumable digital products through Google Play Billing. Product identifiers are gems_50, gems_120, gems_300, gems_700 and gems_1500. Prices and purchase processing are supplied by Google Play. BERSHA GAMES does not receive or store your full payment-card details. Purchase transaction information may be processed by Google Play for billing, fraud prevention, refunds and transaction management.

6. Sharing and third parties

BERSHA GAMES does not intentionally upload locally stored game progress to its own server. Advertising-related data may be processed by Google Mobile Ads, and purchase-related data may be processed by Google Play. Google's handling of that information is governed by Google's applicable policies.

7. Backup and sharing

If you choose to export a game backup, Android may open its standard file picker or share interface so you can select where to save or send the file. The selected destination is handled by Android and the app or service you choose.

8. Retention and deletion

Local game data remains on your device until you delete or reset it. You can remove local game data using the in-game privacy/data controls or Android app storage settings. See the Data Deletion page for details. Google-service data is subject to Google's own retention and deletion practices.

9. Sensitive device permissions

Core gameplay does not require precise location, contacts, camera or microphone access. Advertising and billing SDK components may use identifiers or Android/Google Play services where permitted.

10. Changes

This policy will be updated when the app's services or data practices materially change.
